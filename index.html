Free tools for your job application

  • CV maker: 100 templates, 22 of them ATS-friendly, including the traditional Classic SA layout. Save your CV as a PDF or Word file.
  • Cover letter: written for you from your CV, in the same design.
  • Z83 form for government jobs: the 2021 form, filled in from your CV and signed on the screen.
  • PDF tools: edit a PDF, scan, photos to PDF, PDF to Word, fill and sign, merge, compress and protect your ID copy. No watermark.
  • Private: no account. Your details stay on your phone, and the app works without internet after the first visit.
  • Your language: use the screens in English, Sesotho, isiZulu or Afrikaans.

How it works

  1. Fill in your detailsPersonal details, school, work experience and references. Your SA ID number fills in your date of birth for you.
  2. Choose a templateClassic SA for traditional employers, or a modern design. You can switch at any time.
  3. Add a cover letter and documentsThe cover letter is written for you. Take photos of your ID copy and certificates. Government jobs get a Z83 form.
  4. Save or shareDownload everything as one PDF, or send it straight to WhatsApp or email. The tracker reminds you of closing dates.
