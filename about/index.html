About CV Maker SA

A free CV, cover letter and Z83 maker for South African job seekers, with PDF tools. Made in Welkom, Free State.

What CV Maker SA is

CV Maker SA helps South African job seekers make a professional CV on a phone, without paying and without signing up. You fill in your details once, choose one of 100 templates (22 of them ATS-friendly, and the traditional Classic SA layout that many local employers expect) and save your CV as a PDF or Word file.

It also writes a cover letter from your CV, fills in the Z83 form for government jobs, and has free PDF tools: edit a PDF, scan documents, PDF to Word, fill and sign, merge, compress and protect your ID copy. The screens can be used in English, Sesotho, isiZulu or Afrikaans.

Who makes it

CV Maker SA is designed and developed by Tumelo Vincent Tau in Welkom, Free State, South Africa. It is made to work on a phone with little data: after the first visit the app works without internet, and it can be added to your home screen like any other app.

Your details stay with you

There are no accounts. Your CVs, photos and documents are saved only on your own phone or computer and are never uploaded to us. The full details are in the privacy policy.

How it stays free

The app shows one small card marked "Sponsored" for a local business. Sponsor cards never appear on your CV, cover letter, Z83 or any file you save, and there are no watermarks.
