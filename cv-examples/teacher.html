Teacher or Teacher Assistant CV example

A Teacher or Teacher Assistant CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Dedicated Foundation Phase teacher, registered with SACE, with three years of classroom experience. Patient and creative, with a strong focus on reading and numeracy.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Plan and present lessons according to CAPS
  • Prepare, mark and record assessments
  • Manage the classroom and learner discipline
  • Support learners who struggle and report progress to parents
  • Take part in extra-mural activities
  • Keep learner records up to date

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Lesson planning
  • Classroom management
  • Communication
  • Patience

Also list, if you have them:

  • SACE registration number (required to teach)
  • Qualification (B.Ed, PGCE or diploma) and the phase and subjects you can teach
  • School assistant or tutoring experience

4. Tips for Teacher or Teacher Assistant jobs

  • Teachers must be registered with the South African Council for Educators (SACE). Put your SACE number on your CV.
  • Say which phase and subjects you are qualified for, and your teaching experience, including practice teaching.
  • If you were a school assistant in the Presidential Youth Employment Initiative, list it as work experience. Government teaching posts also need a Z83 form.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Teacher or Teacher Assistant

I would like to apply for the post of Teacher or Teacher Assistant, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I hold a B.Ed in Foundation Phase teaching and I am registered with SACE. During three years at a primary school I taught Grade 2, ran a reading club and kept parents involved, and my learners' reading results improved every term.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
