Farm Worker CV example

A Farm Worker CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Hard-working farm worker with five years of experience on a mixed crop and livestock farm. I can drive a tractor, care for animals and work long hours in all weather.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Plant, water and harvest crops
  • Feed and care for livestock
  • Operate and maintain farm equipment
  • Apply fertiliser and pesticides safely
  • Repair fences and farm structures
  • Sort and pack produce

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Crop and livestock care
  • Tractor driving
  • Physical fitness
  • Reliability

Also list, if you have them:

  • Tractor or farm machinery you can drive, and your licence code
  • Spraying or pesticide safety training
  • Crops and animals you have worked with
  • AgriSETA learnerships or courses

4. Tips for Farm Worker jobs

  • Say which crops and animals you worked with: maize, citrus, vegetables, cattle, sheep, poultry.
  • Machines you can drive (tractor, harvester) are a big plus. Include your licence code.
  • Seasonal and harvest work counts as experience. List each season with the farm and dates.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Farm Worker

I would like to apply for the post of Farm Worker, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

For five years I worked on a mixed farm, planting and harvesting maize, feeding and caring for cattle and maintaining fences and equipment. I can drive a tractor, I have spraying safety training, and I work hard in all weather.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
