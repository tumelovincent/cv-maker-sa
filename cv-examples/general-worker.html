General Worker CV example

A General Worker CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Hard-working and reliable general worker with two years of experience on construction sites and in a warehouse. Physically fit, punctual and used to working safely in a team.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Load and offload deliveries
  • Clean and maintain work areas, tools and equipment
  • Assist artisans and supervisors with daily tasks
  • Move stock and materials safely by hand and with trolleys
  • Dig, carry and prepare materials on site
  • Follow health and safety rules and wear PPE
  • Report damaged equipment and hazards to the supervisor

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Physical fitness
  • Health and safety awareness
  • Basic tool handling
  • Teamwork

Also list, if you have them:

  • Driver's licence (with the code), if you have one
  • Health and safety induction or first aid course
  • Any building, gardening or warehouse experience, even piece jobs

4. Tips for General Worker jobs

  • Show that you are reliable: employers of general workers care most about people who arrive every day and on time. Say it in your profile and let your references confirm it.
  • Write down every kind of physical work you have done, including piece jobs, gardening and helping on building sites, with the dates.
  • Mention safety: that you wear PPE, follow site rules and report hazards. It matters on every site.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of General Worker

I would like to apply for the post of General Worker, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

For the past two years I have worked as a general worker for a building contractor, where I loaded and offloaded materials, kept the site clean and helped the bricklayers every day. I am fit, I follow safety rules and my supervisor can confirm that I was never late.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
