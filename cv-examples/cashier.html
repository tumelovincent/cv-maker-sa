Cashier CV example

A Cashier CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Friendly and accurate cashier with 18 months of experience at a busy supermarket till. Good with cash and card payments, quick on the till and always balanced at the end of my shift.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Process cash, card and voucher payments accurately
  • Scan items and give customers the correct change
  • Balance the till at the start and end of each shift
  • Handle refunds, exchanges and lay-bys according to store policy
  • Pack customer purchases and offer store promotions
  • Keep the till area clean and stocked with packets
  • Report shortages and suspicious transactions to the supervisor

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Cash handling
  • Customer service
  • Point-of-sale (POS) systems
  • Basic maths

Also list, if you have them:

  • Point-of-sale (till) systems you have used
  • Matric with Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy
  • Customer service course, if you did one

4. Tips for Cashier jobs

  • Accuracy is everything: if your till always balanced, say so. Employers look for honest people who handle money carefully.
  • Name the kind of shop and how busy it was (for example "a supermarket with about 400 customers a day").
  • Weekend, holiday and month-end work counts as experience: list it with the dates.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Cashier

I would like to apply for the post of Cashier, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

At a supermarket in Welkom I worked on the till for 18 months, handling cash, card payments, refunds and vouchers. My till balanced at the end of every shift, and I enjoy helping customers quickly and politely, even at month-end when it is busy.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
