Waiter or Waitress CV example

A Waiter or Waitress CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Friendly and energetic waitress with two years of experience in a busy family restaurant. I remember orders accurately, upsell specials and keep my tables happy during the rush.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Welcome guests and show them to their tables
  • Take food and drink orders accurately
  • Serve meals and drinks promptly
  • Explain the menu and specials to guests
  • Process bills and payments
  • Clear and set tables and keep the section clean

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Customer service
  • Teamwork
  • Working under pressure
  • Cash handling

Also list, if you have them:

  • Restaurants or hotels you worked at
  • POS systems you used
  • Food safety or barista training

4. Tips for Waiter or Waitress jobs

  • Show your people skills and that you work well under pressure on busy nights.
  • Mention if you handled your own cash-up or worked the till.
  • Hotel, function and event work counts too: list it with dates.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Waiter or Waitress

I would like to apply for the post of Waiter or Waitress, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

For two years I worked as a waitress at a family restaurant that seats 120 people, taking orders, serving food and drinks and doing my own cash-up. Regular customers asked for my tables, and I stay friendly and quick even on Friday nights.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
