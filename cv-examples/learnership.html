Learnership or Internship CV example

A Learnership or Internship CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Motivated school leaver with matric and a computer course, eager to learn and grow through a learnership. Reliable, quick to learn and comfortable working in a team.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Learn and assist with daily tasks in the department
  • Complete workplace assignments and logbooks
  • Attend training sessions and workshops
  • Help with filing, data capturing and reports
  • Work with mentors and team members on projects
  • Apply theory from my studies in the workplace

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Willingness to learn
  • Computer literate
  • Teamwork
  • Time management

Also list, if you have them:

  • Matric subjects and results
  • Courses, even short ones (computer literacy, first aid)
  • Previous learnerships: SETA, qualification, NQF level and dates
  • Volunteer work and school leadership

4. Tips for Learnership or Internship jobs

  • For learnerships, your education and attitude matter most. Start with your matric subjects and any courses.
  • If you did a learnership before, give the SETA, the qualification and NQF level, and the dates.
  • Read the advert carefully and send exactly what it asks for. See our guide on how to apply for learnerships.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Learnership or Internship

I would like to apply for the post of Learnership or Internship, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I completed matric in 2025 with Business Studies and Mathematical Literacy, followed by a computer literacy course. I volunteer at my church's youth programme, and I am eager to learn a trade and earn a qualification through your learnership.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
