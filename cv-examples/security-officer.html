Security Officer CV example

A Security Officer CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Alert and disciplined security officer, PSIRA registered (Grade C), with three years of experience at shopping centres and an office park. Calm under pressure, with clean records of incident reporting.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Control access at gates and keep the visitors register
  • Patrol the premises and check doors, windows and fences
  • Monitor CCTV cameras and alarm systems
  • Search vehicles and people according to company procedure
  • Write occurrence book (OB) entries and incident reports
  • Respond to alarms and emergencies and call the police when needed
  • Hand over shifts with a full report

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • PSIRA registration
  • Access control
  • Report writing
  • Conflict handling

Also list, if you have them:

  • PSIRA registration number and grade (E, D, C, B or A)
  • Firearm competency certificate, if you apply for armed posts
  • First aid and fire-fighting training
  • Driver's licence (needed for patrol and armed response)

4. Tips for Security Officer jobs

  • Put your PSIRA grade and registration number near the top. Most security employers check it first, and your registration must be current.
  • Name the kinds of sites you guarded (shopping centre, mine, school, office park) and the type of work: access control, patrols, control room, armed response.
  • Mention incidents you handled and reports you wrote, without private details.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Security Officer

I would like to apply for the post of Security Officer, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I am a PSIRA-registered Grade C security officer with three years of experience at a shopping centre and an office park. I did access control, patrols and incident reports, and I am disciplined, punctual and calm when there is trouble.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
