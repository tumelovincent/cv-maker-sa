Data Capturer CV example

A Data Capturer CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Accurate and fast data capturer with a typing speed of 45 words per minute and experience capturing records for a municipality. Careful, focused and comfortable with long hours on the computer.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Capture information accurately on the computer system
  • Check data for errors and correct them
  • Scan and file source documents
  • Prepare simple reports from the captured data
  • Keep information confidential
  • Meet daily capturing targets

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Fast and accurate typing
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Attention to detail
  • Confidentiality

Also list, if you have them:

  • Typing speed (words per minute) and accuracy
  • Systems and programs you used (Excel, SAP, own systems)
  • Computer literacy certificate

4. Tips for Data Capturer jobs

  • Give your typing speed and accuracy. If you do not know it, do a free online typing test first.
  • Mention the kind of data you captured and how much (for example "about 300 forms a day").
  • Accuracy and confidentiality matter: say that you check your work and keep information private.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Data Capturer

I would like to apply for the post of Data Capturer, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

During my year at the municipality I captured about 300 application forms a day into the system, checked them for mistakes and filed the originals. I type 45 words per minute, I work carefully, and I treat people's information as confidential.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
