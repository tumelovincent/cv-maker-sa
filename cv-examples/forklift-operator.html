Forklift Operator CV example

A Forklift Operator CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Certified forklift operator with three years of experience in a busy warehouse. Safe, accurate and experienced on counterbalance forklifts and reach trucks.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Operate the forklift safely to load and offload trucks
  • Move and stack pallets in the warehouse
  • Do daily pre-use checks on the forklift
  • Keep aisles clear and follow safety rules
  • Record stock movements
  • Report damage and faults

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Valid forklift licence
  • Health and safety
  • Stock handling
  • Concentration

Also list, if you have them:

  • Forklift operator certificate, the type of forklift and the date it expires
  • Other machines you can operate
  • Driver's licence

4. Tips for Forklift Operator jobs

  • Put your forklift certificate near the top, with the type of machine (counterbalance, reach truck) and when it expires. Employers will ask to see it.
  • Safety is what employers look for: mention daily pre-use checks and your safety record.
  • Say what you moved and where: loading trucks, racking, cold rooms.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Forklift Operator

I would like to apply for the post of Forklift Operator, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I have a valid forklift operator certificate for counterbalance forklifts and reach trucks, and for three years I loaded trucks and stored stock in high racking at a busy warehouse. I do my pre-use checks every day and had no accidents in three years.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
