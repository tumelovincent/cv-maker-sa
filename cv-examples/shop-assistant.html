Shop Assistant CV example

A Shop Assistant CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Customer-focused sales assistant with experience in clothing retail. I enjoy helping customers find what they need, keep the floor neat and regularly reach my sales targets.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Greet customers and help them find products
  • Explain product features, prices and promotions
  • Receive stock and pack shelves neatly
  • Do stock counts and report shortages
  • Keep the store clean, neat and well displayed
  • Process sales and returns on the till when needed
  • Meet daily and monthly sales targets

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Customer service
  • Sales
  • Product knowledge
  • Communication

Also list, if you have them:

  • The type of shop (clothing, cellphones, hardware, furniture)
  • Sales targets you reached
  • Languages you speak with customers

4. Tips for Shop Assistant jobs

  • Show that you can sell: mention targets you reached or products you are good at explaining.
  • List the languages you speak. In a South African shop that is a real advantage.
  • Seasonal and Black Friday or Christmas work counts as experience.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Shop Assistant

I would like to apply for the post of Shop Assistant, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

At a clothing store I helped customers on the floor, worked the fitting rooms and the till, and kept the displays neat. I reached my monthly sales target most months, and I speak English, Sesotho and isiZulu, which helped me serve many different customers.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
