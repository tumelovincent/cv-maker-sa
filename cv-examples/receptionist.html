Receptionist CV example

A Receptionist CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Friendly and professional receptionist with experience at a medical practice. I answer calls in three languages, manage bookings and make every visitor feel welcome.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Welcome visitors and direct them to the right person
  • Answer and transfer calls on the switchboard
  • Take messages and pass them on accurately
  • Book appointments and manage the visitors register
  • Receive and send deliveries and post
  • Keep the reception area neat

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Telephone etiquette
  • Customer service
  • Computer literate (MS Office)
  • Professional appearance

Also list, if you have them:

  • Switchboard or booking systems you used
  • Languages you speak
  • MS Office skills

4. Tips for Receptionist jobs

  • A receptionist is the first person visitors meet. Show your people skills and your phone manner in your profile.
  • List every language you speak and the systems you used for bookings or the switchboard.
  • A neat, well-presented CV matters for this job: it is a sample of your work.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Receptionist

I would like to apply for the post of Receptionist, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

At a medical practice I welcomed patients, answered a busy switchboard, made bookings and handled payments. I speak English, isiXhosa and Afrikaans, and patients often told the doctor how welcome they felt.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
