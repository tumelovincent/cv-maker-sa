Driver CV example

A Driver CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Safe and reliable driver with a Code EC (Code 14) licence and PrDP, with five years of long-distance and local delivery experience. Clean driving record, good route knowledge and careful with vehicles and goods.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Deliver goods to customers safely and on time
  • Check the vehicle before every trip (tyres, oil, lights)
  • Load and offload goods and check them against invoices
  • Plan routes and keep a trip logbook
  • Collect proof of delivery and payments
  • Report vehicle faults and accidents immediately

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Valid driver's licence and PrDP
  • Route planning
  • Vehicle checks
  • Time management

Also list, if you have them:

  • Driver's licence code (B, EB, C1, C, EC1, EC) and expiry date
  • Professional Driving Permit (PrDP) and its category
  • Clean driving record
  • Vehicles you have driven

4. Tips for Driver jobs

  • Put your licence code and PrDP right at the top. Many transport jobs need a PrDP for goods or passengers, and employers check it first.
  • List the vehicles and routes you know, and whether you did local deliveries or long distance.
  • A clean driving record and taking care of vehicles (daily checks, logbooks) are big selling points.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Driver

I would like to apply for the post of Driver, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I hold a Code EC (Code 14) licence and a PrDP, and for five years I drove trucks for a logistics company, delivering locally and to Gauteng and the Free State. I do my daily vehicle checks, keep accurate trip logs and have a clean driving record.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
