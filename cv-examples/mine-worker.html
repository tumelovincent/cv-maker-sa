Mine Worker CV example

A Mine Worker CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Experienced underground mine worker with six years at a gold mine. Medically fit, trained in mine safety and used to shift work in a team.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Do underground work safely according to mine standards
  • Operate mining equipment such as drills and winches
  • Support and barricade workplaces
  • Do pre-shift safety checks
  • Report unsafe conditions immediately
  • Work as part of a production team to meet targets

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Mine health and safety
  • Equipment operation
  • Teamwork
  • Physical fitness

Also list, if you have them:

  • Certificate of Fitness (medical) for mine work
  • Mine induction and safety training
  • Blasting certificate or machine tickets, if you have them
  • Mines, levels and shafts you worked at

4. Tips for Mine Worker jobs

  • List the mines, the type of mining (gold, platinum, coal) and whether you worked underground or on surface.
  • Mention your tickets and certificates: medical fitness, blasting certificate, machine operation, first aid.
  • Safety is the first thing mines look for: mention your safety record and training.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Mine Worker

I would like to apply for the post of Mine Worker, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

I worked underground at a gold mine for six years as a general miner's assistant, supporting the team with drilling, installing support and keeping the working area safe. I have a valid medical certificate of fitness and a good safety record.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
