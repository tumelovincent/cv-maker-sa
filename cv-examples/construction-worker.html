Construction Worker CV example

A Construction Worker CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Skilled construction worker with five years of experience in bricklaying, plastering and tiling on housing projects. I read building plans, work fast and neatly, and follow site safety rules.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Lay bricks, plaster walls and lay tiles
  • Mix concrete and mortar
  • Read and follow building plans
  • Set up and use scaffolding safely
  • Use hand and power tools correctly
  • Keep the site clean and follow safety rules

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Bricklaying
  • Reading plans
  • Health and safety
  • Teamwork

Also list, if you have them:

  • Trades you can do (bricklaying, plastering, tiling, steel fixing, carpentry)
  • Working at heights or health and safety training
  • Construction learnerships or trade tests
  • Projects you worked on

4. Tips for Construction Worker jobs

  • Name your trades and how experienced you are in each. A list like "bricklaying, plastering, tiling" tells a foreman exactly what you can do.
  • Mention the projects: houses, RDP housing, schools, roads, with the dates.
  • Safety training such as working at heights or first aid makes a difference on sites.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Construction Worker

I would like to apply for the post of Construction Worker, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

For five years I worked on housing and school projects as a bricklayer and plasterer, and I can also tile and read building plans. I work quickly and neatly, I have working-at-heights training and I follow the site safety rules.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
