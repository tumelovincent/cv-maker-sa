Call Centre Agent CV example

A Call Centre Agent CV for South African employers: what to write, the duties and skills to list, a cover letter and tips.

1. An example profile

A short profile at the top of your CV says who you are and what work you want. Change it to fit your own experience:

Patient and well-spoken call centre agent with experience in inbound customer service. I handle up to 80 calls a day, meet my targets and stay calm with difficult customers.

2. Duties to list

Under each job like this in your work experience, list three to five of the duties you really did:

  • Answer inbound calls and help customers with queries
  • Make outbound sales and follow-up calls
  • Capture customer details and notes on the system
  • Resolve complaints or escalate them to a team leader
  • Meet call quality and sales targets
  • Follow scripts and compliance rules

3. Skills and certificates

Skills employers look for in this job:

  • Communication
  • Customer service
  • Computer literate
  • Patience

Also list, if you have them:

  • Languages you speak well
  • Inbound, outbound or sales experience
  • Targets you met and systems you used

4. Tips for Call Centre Agent jobs

  • Say whether you did inbound (customers calling in), outbound or sales calls, and how many calls a day.
  • Targets matter in call centres: mention the ones you met, for example quality scores or sales.
  • Your languages are a big advantage. Many call centres look for agents who speak more than one South African language.

5. An example cover letter

Send a short cover letter with your CV, naming the post and the reference number from the advert:

Dear Hiring Manager

Re: Application for the post of Call Centre Agent

I would like to apply for the post of Call Centre Agent, as advertised. Please find my CV attached.

For a year I worked inbound customer service for an insurance company, handling about 80 calls a day, updating client details and solving queries. I met my quality targets every month and I stay calm and polite, even with angry customers.

I am available for an interview at any time and can be reached on 072 123 4567. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully
Thabo Mokoena
