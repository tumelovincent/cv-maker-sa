PDF to Word for free

Got a PDF you need to change in Word or WPS Office? CV Maker SA turns it into an editable Word file on your phone or computer. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and your file is never uploaded.

Updated 2 November 2026 · By Tumelo Vincent Tau

Turn your PDF into Word – freeChoose a PDF from your phone, WhatsApp, Drive or Downloads, and save the Word file. Nothing to install on a computer, and no account.
PDF to Word now

What comes across

  • Paragraphs come in as paragraphs, so you can type in them and the lines wrap again in Word.
  • Bold, italic, the size of the text and the font names are kept, and centred lines stay centred.
  • Text side by side (columns, simple tables) comes in on one line with tab stops.
  • Pictures, such as a photo or a logo, come in where they were.
  • Scanned pages come in as pictures of the page.
  • Each page of the PDF starts a new page in Word.

How to do it

  1. Open CV Maker SA and tap PDF to Word under PDF tools.
  2. Tap Choose from phone and pick your PDF.
  3. Wait a few seconds, then tap Save to phone or Share. The Word file has the same name as the PDF, ending in .docx.
  4. Open it in Word, WPS Office or Google Docs and make your changes.

Good to know

  • Check it before you send it. Tables lose their lines and special layouts may need tidying in Word.
  • Only want to change a few words? The free PDF editor changes the PDF itself and keeps its look exactly.
  • Your file stays private: the converting happens on your own phone or computer.
