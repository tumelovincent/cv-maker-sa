Edit a PDF for free

Need to fix a mistake in a PDF, change a date or a phone number, or fill in and sign a form? CV Maker SA has a free PDF editor that works on your phone and on a computer. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and your file never leaves your device.

What you can do

  • Change the text that is already there. Tap a line, or a whole paragraph, type the new words and tap Done. The old words are taken out of the PDF and the new ones go in the same place, in the same size and colour, in a matching font (look-alikes of Arial, Times New Roman, Calibri and Cambria, in bold and italic). A changed paragraph wraps by itself, like in Word.
  • Find and replace a word or a date on every page at once.
  • Fill in forms. When a PDF has its own boxes to fill in (bank, school and company forms often do), tap a box and type, tick the boxes and pick from the lists. Other apps see what you filled in.
  • Black out private details, such as an ID number or bank details, before you share a document. The words under the black box are taken out of the file, not just covered.
  • Scanned pages: tap "Read the words on this page" and the words on a scan can be changed too (English).
  • Add new text anywhere, in bold, italic, underlined or crossed out, in five fonts and many colours.
  • Add a picture, such as a logo, a stamp or a photo, and highlight, white out, draw, and add boxes, lines and arrows.
  • Sign with your finger, and add ticks, crosses and today's date.
  • Pages: add pages from another PDF or from photos, add a blank page, add page numbers, turn, move or delete a page.
  • Copy, move and undo: copy anything you added, move it one point at a time with the arrows, and undo any step before you save.
  • Change it later: open the saved PDF again in CV Maker SA and tap "Change what you added" to move, change or delete what you added.

How to edit a PDF on your phone

  1. Open CV Maker SA. Edit PDF is the first thing under PDF tools.
  2. Tap Choose from phone and pick your PDF (from Downloads, WhatsApp or Google Drive). You can also take a photo of a page: it becomes a PDF you can write on.
  3. Tap Edit text, then tap the line you want to change. Choose the whole paragraph or just that line, type the new words and tap Done.
  4. Use the other tools at the bottom to add text, pictures, highlights or your signature. Tap anything you added to move it or change it.
  5. Tap Save. Your file is saved as a new PDF ending in "- edited", so the original stays as it was. Share it on WhatsApp or email straight away.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no trial and no watermark on your pages. The same editor is in the CV Maker SA Android app and on this website, and it works on a computer too.

Your file stays private

The editing happens on your own phone or computer. Your PDF is not uploaded to any server, so it is safe to edit documents such as an ID copy, a payslip or a signed form. Edited files are kept in My files in the app until you delete them.

Good to know

  • Fonts are matched, not copied. Changed text uses the closest of the app's fonts, so it can look slightly different from the original.
  • Scanned pages are pictures: after "Read the words", a changed line is covered and written again on top. The reader knows English best, and handwriting cannot be read.
  • Black-out: when a black box covers a picture or a drawing, that page is saved as a picture, so nothing under the box can be recovered.
  • Locked PDFs: some PDFs are locked against changes. You can still edit them, but they are saved as pictures of the pages.
  • Your signature is a picture of your handwritten signature, which is what most job applications and forms ask for. It is not a certified digital signature.
