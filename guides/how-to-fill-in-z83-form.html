How to fill in the Z83 form for government jobs

Every application for a South African government post needs a Z83 form. Only the new version, effective from 1 January 2021, is accepted, and a blank section or a missing signature can disqualify you.

Updated 24 October 2026 · By Tumelo Vincent Tau

Before you start

  • Use the new Z83. It says "Effective 01 January 2021" at the top. The DPSA vacancy circular tells applicants to use it, and some departments state that applications on the old Z83 will not be considered.
  • Keep the advert open. You need the exact post name, the department and the reference number.
  • Read the notes in the advert. Each department sets its own rules: how to apply (email, online, post or hand delivery), the file format and the closing date. Departments state that late applications will not be considered.

The Z83, section by section

The form says all sections must be completed fully, accurately and legibly. Where a question does not apply to you, write "Not applicable" instead of leaving it blank.

Your details must match your ID. The form explains that race and gender are asked for employment equity.
C. Contact detailsPreferred language and how the department should contact you (post, email, fax or telephone)Use an email address and phone number you check every day.
D. Language proficiencyEach South African language you know, rated good, fair or poor for speaking and for reading/writingBe honest; you may be asked about it in the interview.
E. Formal qualificationsSchool or college, qualification and year, from highest to lowest, and current studiesInclude matric (NSC) and any TVET or university qualification.
F. Work experienceEmployers, post held, dates (MM/YY), reason for leaving, and whether a condition prevents your re-appointment in the Public ServiceAlways answer the re-appointment question, even if you write "see attached CV" for the rest.
G. ReferencesName, relationship to you and office-hours phone numberAsk your references first and check the numbers work.
DeclarationSignature and dateSign and date the form, and initial the pages. Departments disqualify forms that are not signed.

When can you write "see attached CV"?

Departments ask for Parts A, B, C and D to be completed in full. For Parts E, F and G, many accept "see attached CV" when there is not enough space, but only if your CV really contains all the information the form asks for. The question in Part F about conditions that prevent re-appointment must always be answered on the form itself.

What to send with your Z83

  • Your signed Z83 and a detailed CV. Some departments disqualify applications that leave out either one.
  • Certified copies are usually not needed when you apply. The Z83 form says shortlisted candidates will be asked for certified documents, normally on or before the interview day. The form says certified copies should not be older than six months unless the advert says otherwise.
  • Follow the advert if it asks for more. If an advert asks for specific documents with the application, attach them.
  • Applying by email? Some departments ask for everything combined into one PDF and do not accept zipped files or photos.

Mistakes that get applications disqualified

  • Using the old Z83 form
  • Not signing or initialling the form
  • Leaving sections of Parts A to D blank
  • No reference number, or the wrong one
  • No CV attached
  • Sending it after the closing date
