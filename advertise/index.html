Advertise your business on CV Maker SA

Put your business in front of South Africans who are making a CV, with one small sponsor card.

What you get

One small card marked "Sponsored" inside CV Maker SA, shown to people while they make their CV, cover letter or Z83 form. This is what it looks like:

  • Where it shows: on the front page of the app, and as one slim line under the CV preview.
  • Your buttons: up to two, each opening your WhatsApp, phone, email or website.
  • Sharing the space: when there is more than one sponsor, the cards take turns in the same place every few seconds.
  • What you can see: on the website we count how many times your buttons were tapped, and can tell you that number. We never know or share who tapped.

Who sees it

People in South Africa who are looking for work: school leavers, first-time job seekers and working people making a CV on their phone. Ask for the latest visitor numbers when you enquire.

A card suits businesses that help people who are starting out or moving on: training and short courses, printing and internet cafes, driving schools, business registration and tax services, uniforms and workwear, and local shops and services.

What we need from you

  • Your business name.
  • One sentence about what you offer (about 100 characters).
  • The area you serve, if you want it shown.
  • The WhatsApp number, phone number, email address or website for your buttons.
  • A small square logo, if you have one. Without a logo the card shows your initials in a coloured circle.
  • The dates you want the card to run.

What a card never does

  • It never appears on anyone's CV, cover letter, Z83 or any file they save.
  • No pop-ups, no video and no sound.
  • It collects nothing about the people who see it.

Adverts must be honest and legal. We do not accept adverts that ask job seekers to pay for a job, an interview or a learnership.
