Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short
  • Your CVs, cover letters, Z83 forms, photos and documents are saved only on your own phone or computer.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.
  • The Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your details anywhere.

Who we are

CV Maker SA is an app and website for making South African CVs, cover letters and Z83 forms. It is designed and developed by Tumelo Vincent Tau, Welkom, Free State, South Africa. Contact: tumelovincent558@gmail.com.

Information you enter

To make a CV you type in details such as your name, ID number, date of birth, contact details, address, languages, school and work history, references and other details South African CVs often include. You can also add a photo, a signature and copies of documents such as your ID or certificates.

All of this is saved only on your device: inside the app on Android, or in your browser's storage on the website. It is never sent to us or to anyone else by CV Maker SA. We cannot see it.

The Android app

The app does not ask for internet access and has no way to send information over the internet. It asks for these permissions, only for the reasons below:

  • Camera – to take your CV photo or photos of your documents, only when you tap the camera button.
  • Notifications – to remind you of job closing dates you add in the application tracker.
  • Run at start-up – to keep those reminders after your phone restarts.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any website host, GitHub receives standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser loads the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement. The details you type into CV Maker SA are not part of this: they stay in your browser and are never uploaded. After the first visit the website also works without internet.

When you save or share

When you save, share or print a file, it goes only where you choose, for example your Downloads folder, WhatsApp or Gmail. The app you share to handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback, your own email app sends it. We then receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to improve CV Maker SA, we do not share it, and we delete it if you ask. Please do not send your ID number or other private details in feedback.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Delete a CV or document in the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes everything CV Maker SA saved on that device.
  • Backup files you make with "Back up all CVs" are normal files that you control. Keep them somewhere safe.
  • You can turn on a PIN lock in the menu to keep others from opening your CVs.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you have the right to know what personal information is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. CV Maker SA does not hold your CV information, as it stays on your device. For feedback emails or any privacy question, contact tumelovincent558@gmail.com.

Children

CV Maker SA is made for people looking for work. It is not aimed at children.

Not a government app

CV Maker SA is not part of, or endorsed by, the South African government. The Z83 feature helps you fill in the public Z83 application form; you submit your application yourself, in the way the advert asks.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
